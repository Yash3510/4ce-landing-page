# Page Brief: Shopify Editions Winter '26 (The Renaissance Edition)

## Source
- URL: `https://www.shopify.com/editions/winter2026`
- Local URL: `http://localhost:3000/editions/winter2026` (with `/` redirecting to `/editions/winter2026`)

## Design & Theme Overview
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial classical Renaissance artwork juxtaposed with modern tech/streetwear elements (sneakers, laptop, neon pink coffee cup, skateboard, purple superhero mask, neon shopping bag).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Backgrounds: Muted parchment/beige, cosmic deep black, dark navy
  - Accents: Neon pink, violet/purple, cobalt blue, gold/bronze
  - Typography: Dark (#1a1a1a / text-dark) on light backgrounds; crisp white (#ffffff / text-light) on dark scenes
- **Typography:**
  - `NeueMontreal` (Bold 700) - Primary modern grotesque sans-serif for numbers, subheadings, labels, and titles
  - `HWCigars` (Regular 400) - Classical Roman/engraving serif for headers and chapter markings
  - `ImperialScript` (Regular 400) - Renaissance cursive calligraphy script (seen in "ai" ligature in "Renaissance")
